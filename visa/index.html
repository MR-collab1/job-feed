Australian visa & permanent residency statistics

Visa grants, permanent migration program outcomes and citizenship conferrals, built from Department of Home Affairs data published on data.gov.au. Checked every day; the charts change whenever Home Affairs publishes.

Sample data — these are not real statistics. You are viewing ?demo=1, which loads made-up numbers so the layout can be reviewed before the pipeline has run. Switch to the published data.

Sources

Every figure above is traceable to one of these published files. Follow a link to the dataset on data.gov.au to read the department's own notes, definitions and caveats before quoting a number.

    This is an independent reuse of Australian public-sector open data. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official channel of the Department of Home Affairs. Figures are counts of visas granted in a period, not applications lodged, people arrived, or the current stock of visa holders.

    Home Affairs publishes these statistics on a monthly to annual cycle depending on the dataset, so the underlying numbers do not change daily even though this site checks for new releases daily. Counts below a small threshold are suppressed at the source for privacy and are treated here as unknown, not zero, so category totals may not sum to a published grand total.
